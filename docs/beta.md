# Waquela Beta Notes

Waquela Beta is an **experimental service**. Access and features may be limited during the Beta period. Broad public recruitment is on hold; an accessible website does not guarantee full service for every visitor.

## Before You Use It

- Features may change.
- Availability is not guaranteed; the service may be interrupted or discontinued.
- Do not use the Beta for critical tasks.
- Avoid sensitive information. Use non-sensitive test content.
- AI tool availability and behavior vary by client and setup.
- There is no guarantee of permanent storage, unlimited capacity, or free service forever.

## Private Information and Public Publications

Private Cards require appropriate permission. Publishing a selected snapshot makes that snapshot publicly accessible, including to people who are not signed in.

Withdrawal stops availability through Waquela but does not erase historical records or copies already obtained. Review your information before publishing it.

## Transaction Offices

A personal Office receives external submissions into its owner's private Inbox. Visitor identity and reply methods are self-declared and unverified.

Submissions require Human review. They do not automatically modify Cards or establish trusted relationships. Do not submit sensitive information through an Office.

**Known limitation:** an explicit Mixed Inbox V2 request returned a legacy projection through an AI connection. The production cause remains unresolved, and ChatGPT Mixed Inbox V2 production acceptance is incomplete. Do not assume that an AI-provided Inbox list includes every incoming entry.
